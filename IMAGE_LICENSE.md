# Third-party images

The MIT licence covers this project's original code, documentation and benchmark annotations. It does not grant rights to third-party images, including image content shown in project figures.

We do not redistribute the benchmark image files. Instead, the repository provides a download script and reconstruction settings in `data/images/derivations.json`. Downloaded and reconstructed images remain subject to their original licences and applicable source terms; transformations do not relicense them.

Source links, authors and per-image licence references are recorded in [`data/images/sources.csv`](data/images/sources.csv). Please follow those terms, including any attribution or redistribution requirements. Pexels images follow the [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/); Wikimedia Commons images follow the licence specified on each source page.
